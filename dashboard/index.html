The model, stated whole

implied = exp(a + b × ln TVL) — one fitted size curve, comps-2

  • The fit. Least squares of ln market cap on ln TVL across the snapshot's traded protocols, cleaned three ways: exchange (CEX) listings dropped ( of traded), one row kept per ticker family ( duplicates dropped), nothing else touched. What remains: n = , intercept a = , slope b = .
  • Bands. The fit set is resampled with replacement and refitted 200 times — seed fixed, so the board is reproducible to the dollar. Each row's band is the p10–p90 of its 200 refit prices, its rank range the p10–p90 of its refit ranks. A pure size curve ranks by size, so the refits move the values, not the order.
  • Selection. The competition's own rule, whole: no token recorded in the snapshot (no market cap, no ticker, no listing id) and no version marker in the name. The one exclusion kept is CEX — a centralised exchange is a company, not a protocol launch. Bridges, staking, restaking: swept in full.
  • Netting. Every farm plan ends in a verdict on stated assumptions: at a launch, % of the protocol's implied value at its band low goes to the community, and one wallet captures % of that pool. A plan pays for itself if the launch chance beats its all-in cost divided by that reward — FARM at or under %, THIN at or under %, otherwise SKIP.

Tested out of sample

Typical miss — the median pricing error, as a multiple — measured two ways on the cleaned fit set: leave-one-out (each protocol priced by a model fitted without it) and a time split (the older half by listing date fits; the newer half is priced blind). Lower is better; every model here is wrong by a lot, and the table says by how much.

Terms on trial

Four extra terms were fitted and put through the same two tests. A term ships only if it improves leave-one-out without worsening the time split. None did — the shipped model is the pure curve; the sandbox switches two of them back on to show what they would do.

The sandbox — break it yourself

Move a slider and the board re-prices and re-ranks in place. The slope rotates the fitted line around its mean point; the two weights revive terms the test rejected, at the strength you choose — see which conclusions are robust and which hang on one constant.

Implied values are a model's reading of one frozen snapshot, not a promise about any launch. Farm plans are starting points from public data; a protocol's own page is the only authority on its terms. Every view has a deep link — #/board, #/detail/<slug>, #/market, #/model — and the board's state rides along.
