The model, stated whole

implied = TVL × category multiple × momentum × maturity × reach

  • Category multiple. The median market-cap/TVL of the category's live peers, blended toward the global median in log space with weight n/(n+K), K = , clamped to [, ]. No peers: the global median stands in.
  • Momentum. 7-day TVL change, clamped to ±%. Gains count at weight, losses at — money leaving is harder to argue with than money arriving.
  • Maturity. + × age in days / . Time on DefiLlama is survival evidence; an unknown age reads as days.
  • Reach. + per chain beyond the first, up to extra. Distribution is a launch asset.
  • Selection. One rule for the whole board: no token recorded, no version marker in the name, not a wrapped, staked or bridged product, not an exchange front. The house watchlist is covered in full, then the board keeps going to the snapshot's $5M edge.

No protocol is named anywhere in the model: the same function prices Steakhouse and the smallest farm on the board, and every farm plan is written by the same playbook logic from the protocol's own category, chain, size and age.
